=== GothicSigilCursor Cursor Set ===

By: Yoko

Download: https://www.rw-designer.com/cursor-set/gothicsigilcursor

Author's description:

Gothic Sigil Cursor is a stark, high-contrast theme that abandons color for pure form. Each icon is meticulously crafted to resemble a carved stone relief or a sacred geometric diagram. Perfect for those who appreciate order, sharp aesthetics, and a touch of arcane mystery.

🏛️ Sacred Geometry — Features intricate tessellations, tridents, and gothic arches.
🎨 Monochrome Mastery — Stark black-on-white design ensures maximum visibility and a clean UI.
🖱️ Plug & Play — Standard `.cur` / `.ani` formats, no extra software required.
🎯 Dual-Purpose Icons — Carefully designed to be legible at both tiny (32px) and large (48px+) sizes.
